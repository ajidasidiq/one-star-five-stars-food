Entry for the make.fun bounty "One Star, Five Stars for the Food": one
sentence, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an alien tourist giving
planet Earth a one-star review while still slipping in a real compliment
about the food.

## The entry

```
One star: your species pays for water, worships glowing rectangles, and queues for bitter bean juice — but the dumplings were so good I nearly defected and opened a stall.
```

## Why this line

The grievance is an outsider's bafflement at three very human habits —
paying for water, worshipping glowing rectangles, queuing for bitter
bean juice (coffee) — and the turn lands on the food: the dumplings were
so good the tourist nearly defected and opened a stall, which is both the
compliment and the punchline.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
